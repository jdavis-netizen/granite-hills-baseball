New Player Sign-Up

Playing this fall or winter, or trying out in February? Start here.

Anyone who wants to play baseball at Granite Hills — this fall and winter with Eagle Baseball, or for the school season in February — fill out the sign-up form so the coaches have your contact info and your parents'. It takes about two minutes. You'll get information about fall ball, tryouts, and team communication.

Player Sign-Up Form

Player info, positions, parent contact, and your AAU status for fall ball.

Open the Sign-Up Form → Opens in a new tab. Have your student ID number handy.

Who should fill this out

  • Anyone who wants to play fall and winter ball with Eagle Baseball
  • Anyone who wants to try out in February for the school team
  • Incoming freshmen and students new to Granite Hills
  • Returning players who haven't given the coaches a current phone and email

Playing fall ball?

Fall practices and games run as Eagle Baseball, an AAU club. Every player needs a $24 AAU membership before the first practice. The sign-up form asks where you are on that; the instructions are on the Fall Ball Sign-Up page.

Transferring in?

If you played at another high school last year, CIF transfer paperwork has to be filed before you can compete. Tell us your previous school on the form and Coach Davis will walk you through the 510 process.
