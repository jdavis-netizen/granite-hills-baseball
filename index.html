New Player Meeting

For any student who wants to play baseball at Granite Hills: fall ball, winter workouts, or tryouts in February. The meeting is at lunch in Coach Davis's classroom, Room 40.

Latest News

Summer 2026

Two Eagles Commit to Division I Programs

Noah Figuered ('24) is headed to Oregon after setting the NWAC record with 74 stolen bases at Edmonds College, and Owen Rodgers ('24) committed to USD following a First Team All-PCAC season at Grossmont College.

May 2026

Windham Hits .495 — 4th Best in Program History

Freshman Colton Windham batted .495 with 45 hits, 32 RBI, and 11 doubles — the 4th-best single-season average in Granite Hills history — while adding 4 saves and a 0.84 ERA out of the bullpen.

May 14, 2026

Record 5th Straight League Title

A 13-0 Senior Day shutout of Valhalla clinched the Eagles' fifth consecutive Grossmont Hills League championship — a league record, breaking Helix's mark of four straight.
